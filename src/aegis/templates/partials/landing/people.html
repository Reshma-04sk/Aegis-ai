For the people you worry about

  • A child with football after school

    Moved to the cleanest hour before dark, or swapped for an indoor game when even that hour is unhealthy.

  • A grandparent who walks every morning

    Winter mornings are often the smokiest hours. AEGIS points to a cleaner slot later in the day, or a walk indoors.

  • A runner with asthma

    Hard exercise counts one step stricter, because you breathe several times more air while running. Inhaler reminders included.

  • Someone expecting a baby

    Treated as a sensitive group throughout, with indoor air steps first and outdoor time kept to the cleanest hours.
