One plan, four decisions

  • A call for each person

    Asthma, a child, an older parent, a pregnancy or a heart condition each make the call stricter. The household follows its most careful member.

  • The right hour for each outing

    The walk, the match, the errand: each lands in its cleanest forecast hours, with the PM2.5 you skip by waiting.

  • Cleaner air indoors

    Is the purifier big enough for the room, how fast it clears the air, and the single hour worth opening a window.

  • A greener trip, honestly

    Carbon saved by switching how you travel, next to the smoke you would breathe doing it. On bad days these pull apart, and Aegis shows both.
