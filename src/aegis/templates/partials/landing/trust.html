What the numbers are, and are not

Forecast

A model estimate on a regional grid. It covers places with no monitor and looks ahead, so it drives the plan. Always labelled as an estimate.

Measurement

The nearest official monitor that reported in the last {{ config.stationRecentHours }} hours, exactly as its operator publishes it, with distance and age. Shown beside the forecast, never blended in.

Age and gaps

Every value shows how old it is, and anything older than {{ config.staleAfterHours }} hours is marked stale. A missing reading stays missing; nothing is filled in.
