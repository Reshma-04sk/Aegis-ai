One plan for everyone at home

Who should go out
A verdict for each person, stricter for asthma, children, older adults, pregnancy and heart conditions. The household follows the most careful one.
When to go
Each walk, game or errand moved to its cleanest forecast hours, with the PM2.5 you avoid breathing by waiting.
Air at home
Whether your purifier is big enough for the room, how fast it cleans the air, and the one hour worth opening a window.
Getting around
The carbon each way to travel emits, shown next to the smoke you would breathe on the way. On smoggy days the two disagree, and AEGIS says so.
