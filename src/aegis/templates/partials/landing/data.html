Forecast and measurement, side by side

Forecast

A model estimate from Copernicus CAMS on a regional grid, for the next 24 hours. It covers places with no monitor nearby, so it drives the planning. Always labelled as an estimate.

Measured

The nearest official monitor within {{ config.stationRadiusKm }} km that has reported in the last {{ config.stationRecentHours }} hours, exactly as its operator publishes it, with its distance and age. Shown beside the forecast, never blended into it.

Anything older than {{ config.staleAfterHours }} hours is marked stale. Missing readings stay missing.
