How Aegis decides

  1. Sense

    The next 24 hours of the Copernicus CAMS forecast for your spot, and the nearest reference monitor within {{ config.stationRadiusKm }} km, such as a CPCB station.

  2. Decide

    Published EPA categories and health guidance set every call, identically every time. No model takes part.

  3. Explain

    Google Gemini rewrites the finished plan in plain words. It sees no names and cannot alter a call or a number.
